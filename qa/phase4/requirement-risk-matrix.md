# Requirement and risk coverage matrix

Snapshot: 1 November 2026, retained Phase 2 backend and Phase 3 frontend evidence. This Phase 4 document does not represent a new execution. IDs from [FE requirements](../requirements.md), [BE requirements](../../../plutus-be/qa/requirements.md) and their defect registers are preserved. Added `RISK-*` rows organize existing evidence and gaps; they are not newly approved business requirements.

**Covered** means the named assertions passed within their stated doubles or real boundary. **Failed** means an observed failed gate or assertion, including tooling failure. **Blocked** means an unmet prerequisite prevented execution. **Unimplemented** means no applicable executable scenario/runner is established here. **Pending** means a real human has not supplied review or UAT. A blocked gate can also have an unimplemented downstream runner. None of these statuses implies release approval.

`Unit/component` includes actual React components with service/auth/navigation doubles and backend logic with model/provider doubles. `HTTP/integration` means the real boundary explicitly named. `Browser` below is the real Next development route with mocked auth/domain APIs; it is not a paired FE/BE system. `Real system` requires actual FE-to-BE-to-owned-database operation. All human results remain blank in the [human-validation workbook](human-validation.md).

## Unresolved decisions and independent validation

Keep stable IDs in the [FE defect register](../defects.md) and [BE defect register](../../../plutus-be/qa/defects.md): FE-ROUTE-01, FE-REDIRECT-01, FE-HYDRATE-01 and FE-TOOL-01 are distinct; BE-STORAGE-01 is metadata/durability evidence; BE-RULE-01 remains an unresolved financial requirement. WIP/full-check failures and isolation blockers remain visible. No product fixes or verified resolution are claimed by this matrix.

Next independent validation should review the exact source/evidence and proposed workflow gates first, then repeat authorized isolated local gates with fresh run IDs. Real protected HTTP and paired-stack validation require the containment/provider prerequisite and approved fixtures before execution. Named humans then perform the [UAT scripts](human-validation.md) against the recorded build and provide real feedback. Hosted CI and actual CD require their own authority and observations; these documents do not grant it.
